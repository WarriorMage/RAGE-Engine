## 0. The setting

- **Event:** HackLiffey 2026, a 24-hour student hackathon.
- **Venue:** Liffey Innovation Hub, 14 Bow Street, Smithfield, Dublin 7 (part of Dalton University).
- **Runs:** Saturday 2026-10-03 10:00 to Sunday 2026-10-04 10:00. Judging is Sunday 09:00.
- **Victim team:** Team 7, "Lumen" (Aoife Brennan and Rohan Mehta), at table **T-07** in the main hall.
- **Stolen items:**
  1. **Laptop:** silver ThinkPad X1 Carbon, university asset tag **LT-7731**. It held the only copy of the source code; the GitHub remote was never pushed.
  2. **Prototype:** the "Lumen Band" wearable, serial **LB-PROTO-03**, kept in a grey hard-shell case.
- **Garda incident reference:** **DMR-26-10418**.
- **Weather:** dry until about 01:15, then steady rain until about 04:00.
- **Times** are local Dublin time.

## 1. What really happened

**The thief is Marcus Whelan**, partner at the sponsor Brightwave Ventures.

**Method:**
1. At the 20:30 sponsor preview, Aoife tells Marcus the code is "only on this laptop, we haven't pushed it yet".
2. Marcus was on the "partners" mailing list, so he received the facilities email (EMAIL-001) saying the main-hall camera **CAM-02** would be offline for a firmware update between 01:00 and 02:00 on Sunday.
3. His sponsor visitor badge **HB-9042** has access to the east loading door **DOOR-E2** (for sponsor equipment), and sponsor badges stay active until Sunday 10:00 (EMAIL-002).
4. He leaves visibly through the front door at 23:31 so people remember him going, drives the rented grey Audi A4 **231-D-55120** to his hotel (Harbourview Hotel, Hanover Quay, Grand Canal Dock), switches his phone off at about 01:21, and drives back.
5. He enters through DOOR-E2 at 01:43 with his own badge while CAM-02 is offline, the security guard is outside on a phone call, and the hall monitor is asleep.
6. He takes the laptop and the grey case from T-07, leaves at 01:59, drives off at 02:03, and makes a 4-minute call to a UK number at 02:05. The laptop's last "Find My Device" ping is at 02:21 near Hanover Quay.

**Motive (NEVER recorded in any document):** Brightwave has quietly invested in Pulsewise, a startup building an almost identical wearable. A public, open-sourced Lumen Band winning the prize would wreck Pulsewise's next funding round. Marcus took the code to hand to Pulsewise and to stop the demo. **No document mentions Pulsewise or any motive.**

## 4. Evidence plan

### 4.1 Three contradictions between witnesses

**C1. Was the security desk staffed at about 01:30?**
- Tomás (STMT-005) says he never left SEC-DESK between 00:00 and 03:00.
- Gráinne (STMT-006) says the desk was empty when she passed at about 01:30.
- **Truth:** Tomás was outside from 01:24 to 01:51 on a call. This is resolved by CCTV-001, ACCESS-003 and PHONE-002, and he admits it in INTV-001.

**C2. When was Ciarán at Team 7's table?**
- Priya (STMT-004) says she saw Ciarán at T-07 "around half one".
- Ciarán (STMT-003) says he was in LAB-2 continuously from 00:30 to 02:30 and never went near T-07 after the 21:10 argument.
- **Truth:** both are partly wrong. He was at T-07 at **00:16** to borrow a cable (CHAT-001 photo timestamp, CCTV-002, STMT-002), and he left LAB-2 only for 01:12–01:19 to use WC-1F (ACCESS-002, CCTV-005). He admits the 00:16 visit in INTV-002.

**C3. Who got out of the car in the car park at 01:40?**
- Rohan (STMT-002, INTV-003): "a dark grey saloon, a tall older man in a puffy jacket, maybe one of the sponsors".
- Femi (STMT-008): "a black hatchback, a young guy in a dark hoodie, average height".
- **Truth:** a grey Audi A4 saloon with a tall figure in a quilted jacket (CCTV-004 ANPR and video). Rohan is closer to the truth.

### 4.2 The thief's lie, disproved only by combining a CCTV log and a door log

- **The lie:** Marcus (STMT-009, INTV-004) says that after leaving at 23:31 he drove straight to his hotel, slept, and did not come back until 09:00.
- **ACCESS-001** shows badge HB-9042 granted at DOOR-E2 at 01:43:12. Marcus's badge number is confirmed in INTV-004 and EMAIL-002. On its own this is not proof: Marcus claims in INTV-004 that he lost the badge that night.
- **CCTV-003** shows a tall figure in a navy quilted jacket entering from DOOR-E2 at 01:43:06. On its own it is anonymous: no face is visible.
- **Together** (with the 6-second clock offset from FOREN-002) they show his badge being used by someone matching his clothes (CCTV-001 at 23:31, STMT-007), at the same second.
- CCTV-004 and RCPT-002 strengthen it: his rented Audi is in the car park from 01:39 to 02:03.

### 4.3 Two red herrings

**R1. Ciarán Doyle**
- Why he looks suspicious: he had a motive (the "copied idea" argument at 21:10), he lied about being at T-07, and Priya puts him there at "half one".
- Why he is innocent: the DOOR-L2 log and CAM-05 place him on the first floor for the whole theft window (01:19–02:34).

**R2. Gráinne O'Shea**
- Why she looks suspicious: she takes a big wheeled bin out of DOOR-E2 at 02:14, right after the theft, and Tomás points the finger at her in STMT-005.
- Why she is innocent: the skip was checked and held only pizza boxes and cardboard (NOTE-002, FOREN-002), and the theft was over by 01:59.

### 4.4 Identifying the thief needs at least 3 documents

No single document names him. The chain is:
1. EMAIL-002 or INTV-004: HB-9042 is Marcus's badge.
2. ACCESS-001: HB-9042 used at DOOR-E2 at 01:43:12.
3. CCTV-003: a navy quilted jacket figure enters at the same moment and leaves with the grey case.
4. CCTV-001 and STMT-007: Marcus was wearing a navy quilted jacket that night.
5. CCTV-004 and RCPT-002: Audi 231-D-55120, rented to Marcus, is in the car park from 01:39 to 02:03.
6. PHONE-003: his phone is off from 01:21 to 02:04, and the 02:05 call is made from the Smithfield cell near the hub.
7. EMAIL-004 and RCPT-002: the laptop's last ping is at Hanover Quay, which is the address of his hotel.

### 4.5 Three facts never recorded in any document (the unanswerable questions)

1. **U1:** Where the laptop and the prototype are now. The only known point is the last ping at 02:21; they were never recovered.
2. **U2:** What was said in the 02:05 call to +44 7700 900418, and who that number belongs to. The UK subscriber request is still pending.
3. **U3:** The thief's motive or who he was working for. Pulsewise is never mentioned anywhere.
