My name is Declan Shaw and I am the night porter at the Harbourview Hotel on Hanover Quay. I work the front desk from 22:00 to 07:00. My phone number is 089 771 2045.

On Saturday 3 October, at about 23:55, a guest I recognised as Mr Whelan from room 412 came in through the lobby. He was wearing a navy padded jacket and had a laptop bag on his shoulder. He nodded to me and took the lift.

At about 01:20 on Sunday morning the same guest came back down to the lobby. He was now wearing a dark baseball cap and carrying a black backpack, which looked empty. He did not say anything. He went out the side door to the hotel car park. A minute or two later I saw a grey Audi leave the car park on the camera monitor at the desk.

At about 02:20 the grey Audi came back into the car park. Mr Whelan came in through the side door a few minutes later. The backpack looked full and heavy, and he was carrying a grey plastic case in his other hand. He went straight to the lift.

I did not think much of it at the time. Guests come and go at all hours. I only remembered it when a Garda phoned the hotel on Monday asking about the car park cameras.

I am willing to give a formal statement and the hotel car park footage should still be available.
