My name is Rohan Mehta, phone 085 220 9184, badge HB-1008. I am a member of Team 7 with Aoife Brennan.

Around a quarter past midnight Ciarán Doyle from Team 3 came over to our table, T-07. Earlier that evening he had argued with Aoife, so I was surprised, but he was polite. He asked to borrow a USB-C cable and I gave him one. He was only there for a minute.

At about 00:50 I went up to LAB-2 on the first floor to use the soldering station to fix a loose sensor lead. I left the laptop and the grey prototype case on T-07, because Aoife had already gone to sleep and the hall was quiet. Ciarán and two people from his team were also in LAB-2.

At about 01:20 I ordered snacks on the Slice of Dublin app. The driver rang me at about 01:35 and said he was at the back car park, not the front. So I went down the back stairs and out the east loading door, DOOR-E2, at about 01:38. I met the driver at his car, a white car with a Slice of Dublin sign on the roof.

While I was paying, another car drove into the car park and went down to the far dark corner by the bins. It was a dark grey saloon. A man got out. He was tall and looked older, maybe forties, in a puffy or quilted jacket. To be honest, my first thought was that he looked like one of the sponsors. I only saw him for a few seconds in the rain and I did not see his face.

I went back in through DOOR-E2 with my badge at about 01:41. I checked the door clicked shut behind me. Then I went straight back up to LAB-2 with the food. I stayed in LAB-2 until around 03:15, then slept on a sofa on the first floor.

Aoife rang me at about 07:06 to say the laptop and prototype were gone.

Signed: Rohan Mehta
