INTERVIEW TRANSCRIPT. Incident ref DMR-26-10418.
Location: Bridewell Garda Station, Interview Room 2. Start 15:00, end 15:44.
Present: Det. Garda Sinéad Walsh (SW), Garda Paul Hennessy (PH), Marcus Whelan (MW). The interview is voluntary; MW was offered and declined a solicitor.

SW: Mr Whelan, thank you for coming in. This is a routine interview; we are speaking to everyone who held a badge at HackLiffey. Can you confirm your name and the badge you were issued?
MW: Marcus Whelan, Brightwave Ventures. I had a visitor badge. The number was HB-9042, I believe.

SW: That matches the organisers' register. Where is that badge now?
MW: I don't know. I couldn't find it on Sunday morning. I assumed I had left it in the hub, or dropped it in the car park. I meant to tell Niamh.

SW: When did you last have it?
MW: Saturday evening. I would have had it on my lanyard while I was in the building.

SW: Did you lend it to anyone?
MW: No.

SW: Tell me about the sponsor preview at Team 7's table.
MW: It was around 20:30. Team 7 were the strongest wearable entry. I asked the usual questions: the technology, the market, whether the code was open source. I asked if it was on GitHub. The team lead, Aoife, said it was only on her laptop for now. I said something like "back that up".

SW: What time did you leave the hub?
MW: Around 23:30. I said goodnight to Niamh and to the security guard and left by the front door.

SW: What were you wearing?
MW: A navy quilted jacket. Dark trousers. I had my laptop bag.

SW: How did you travel?
MW: I had a rental car, a grey Audi A4, parked in the rear car park. I walked round the side of the building to it and drove to the Harbourview Hotel on Hanover Quay.

PH: Do you know the registration?
MW: Not offhand. It's a hire car.

PH: The rental agreement in your name lists 231-D-55120. Does that sound right?
MW: If that's what it says.

SW: What did you do at the hotel?
MW: Went to my room, answered a few emails, went to sleep.

SW: Did you leave the hotel at any point during the night?
MW: No.

SW: Did you return to the Liffey Innovation Hub at any point between leaving at 23:30 and arriving for judging on Sunday morning?
MW: No. I arrived just before nine.

SW: Did anyone else drive that car on Saturday night?
MW: No. I'm the only named driver.

SW: Were you on your phone during the night?
MW: I may have been. Emails, a call or two. I work with people in different time zones.

SW: Who would you have been calling?
MW: Colleagues, contacts. I'd rather not go into my business contacts without knowing why.

SW: Do you own a black backpack?
MW: I own several bags. I don't see the relevance.

SW: Were you aware that the main hall camera would be offline between 01:00 and 02:00 on Sunday?
MW: I get a great deal of email from event organisers. I couldn't tell you what was in every one.

SW: Were you aware that your sponsor badge could open the east loading door?
MW: I was told sponsors could bring equipment in through the back. I didn't use it.

SW: Mr Whelan, is there anything else you'd like to tell us about Saturday night?
MW: Only that I hope the team gets their work back. It was a good project.

SW: We may need to speak to you again. Interview concluded at 15:44.
