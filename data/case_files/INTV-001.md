INTERVIEW TRANSCRIPT. Incident ref DMR-26-10418.
Location: Bridewell Garda Station, Interview Room 2. Start 11:00, end 11:41.
Present: Det. Garda Sinéad Walsh (SW), Garda Paul Hennessy (PH), Tomás Ryan (TR). The interview is voluntary; TR is not under arrest.

SW: Tomás, thanks for coming in. For the tape, can you confirm your name and role?
TR: Tomás Ryan. Night security at the Liffey Innovation Hub, badge HB-0050.

SW: In your statement yesterday you said you were at the security desk the whole time from midnight to three. Do you still say that?
TR: Yeah. More or less.

SW: More or less?
TR: I was at the desk.

PH: We have reviewed the CCTV from the front lobby camera, CAM-01. At 01:24:28 a man in a hi-vis jacket with a phone to his ear walks out of the front door, DOOR-M1. The door record shows a push-to-exit at 01:24:30. At 01:51 the same man comes back in, and your badge, HB-0050, opens the front door at 01:51:05. Is that you?
TR: (pause) Yeah. That's me.

SW: So you were away from the desk for about twenty-seven minutes.
TR: I'm sorry. I should have said it yesterday. I was afraid I'd lose the job. My partner, Michelle, rang... no, I rang her. We've been having a hard time and she was upset. I went out the front so I wouldn't wake anyone. I stood under the canopy by the bike racks.

SW: What number did you ring?
TR: Her mobile, 086 118 4410. You can check my phone. I'll give you consent.

PH: We'd appreciate that. Your phone records will show the call start and end times?
TR: They will. It was a long one, I know that.

SW: While you were standing outside the front door, could you see the rear car park?
TR: No. The front is on Bow Street. The car park is round the back, you can't see it from the front at all.

SW: Did you see or hear any car arrive or leave?
TR: Cars go up and down Bow Street all night. Nothing I'd remember. It was lashing rain from about quarter past one.

SW: Did anyone come in the front door while you were outside?
TR: No. Nobody passed me. And the front needs a badge after ten, so no one could just walk in.

SW: What about the east loading door at the back, DOOR-E2? How is that controlled?
TR: Badge to get in, push button to get out. It's the door the cleaner uses and the delivery lads at night. Sponsors could use it as well this weekend, for their kit. Niamh told me that on Saturday.

SW: Can you see DOOR-E2 from the desk?
TR: There's a monitor at the desk for the cameras. I can see the corridor camera, CAM-03, on it. But I wasn't at the desk, so I didn't see it.

PH: Is the camera footage watched live by anyone else when you're away?
TR: No. It's just recorded. It's just me on nights.

SW: In your statement you mentioned the cleaner, Gráinne O'Shea, and her bin. Why did you mention her?
TR: Because she was in and out of the back door with that big bin around quarter past two. I saw her on the monitor once I was back. I just thought it should be looked at. I've nothing against her.

SW: Did you see anything in the bin?
TR: No. Just the bin, lid closed.

SW: Let's go back to earlier. You said in your statement that a sponsor, Mr Whelan, left at about 23:30.
TR: Yeah, tall fella, posh jacket, navy, the padded kind. Green lanyard. He said "goodnight, back at nine". Friendly enough.

SW: Did you see him again before the morning?
TR: Not that I saw. But like I said, I wasn't at the desk for that half hour.

PH: When you came back in at 01:51, did you look at the monitors?
TR: I looked at them. The main hall camera was off. Niamh had warned us about that, a firmware thing, one to two o'clock. The corridor one looked empty when I sat down.

SW: Did the main hall camera come back on at two?
TR: A bit after. Ten past maybe. I didn't look at the tables on it. You can't really make out what's on the tables with the lights down.

SW: Your 03:00 patrol. Tell me exactly what you did.
TR: Walked the ground floor, lobby, kitchen, toilets, the main hall. In the hall I walked down the middle aisle with my torch pointed at the floor so I wouldn't wake people. I didn't look at each table. I wouldn't have known what was supposed to be on Team 7's table anyway.

SW: Is there anything else you haven't told us?
TR: No. That's it. I'm sorry about the desk. I should have been honest from the start.

SW: Thank you, Tomás. Interview concluded at 11:41.
