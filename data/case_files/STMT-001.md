My name is Aoife Brennan. I am a final-year engineering student at Dalton University and the lead of Team 7, "Lumen", at HackLiffey 2026 in the Liffey Innovation Hub, 14 Bow Street. My phone number is 087 412 5531 and my event badge is HB-1007. My teammate is Rohan Mehta.

Our project is the Lumen Band, a wrist wearable that warns people with epilepsy of early seizure signs. We brought one working prototype, serial LB-PROTO-03, which we keep in a grey hard-shell case. All our code was on my silver ThinkPad X1 Carbon, university asset tag LT-7731. I set up a GitHub repository on Saturday but I never pushed to it. The laptop held the only copy.

At about 20:30 on Saturday the sponsors did a preview walk around the tables. A man from Brightwave Ventures, Marcus Whelan, spent around ten minutes at our table, T-07. He was very interested. He asked if the code was on GitHub. I told him, "No, it's only on this laptop, we haven't pushed it yet." He laughed and said we should be careful with that.

At about 21:10 Ciarán Doyle from Team 3 came over and accused us of copying his team's idea. He also complained we were hogging the power strip. It got heated for two or three minutes and then he walked off. I want to be fair: I do not know that he had anything to do with this.

At about 00:40 I was exhausted. I closed the laptop lid (it locks with a password) and left it on T-07 with the grey case beside it. Our cable lock was in my bag but I did not use it. We also had locker L-14 in the lobby and we did not use that either. I went upstairs to the quiet room and fell asleep.

I woke at about 06:55. When I got to T-07 at about 07:05, the laptop and the grey case were gone. The charger was still plugged in and lying on the table. I rang Rohan straight away, then went to the security desk and told the guard, Tomás Ryan, at about 07:08. Niamh Kavanagh, the organiser, came down soon after.

Signed: Aoife Brennan
