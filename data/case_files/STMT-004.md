My name is Priya Nair, phone 083 118 6620, badge HB-1012. I am on Team 5 and our table, T-05, is across the aisle from Team 7's table, T-07.

At around 21:10 I saw Ciarán Doyle from Team 3 having a go at Aoife Brennan at T-07. He was waving his arms and saying they had copied his idea. Everybody near us went quiet. It lasted maybe three minutes. My team joked about it in our group chat.

Later on I saw Ciarán at Team 7's table again. I would say it was around half one, maybe a bit earlier. He was standing at their table with the other Team 7 guy, Rohan, and leaning over it. I remember thinking it was strange because of the argument. I took a quick photo for our group chat as a joke. I did not see him take anything.

The lights in the main hall were dimmed for quiet hours at about half twelve, and it got very dark at the tables.

I am not completely sure of the times. I had been awake since six o'clock Saturday morning and I was very tired. I went up to the quiet room on the first floor at some point and slept there until about 07:15.

When I came down in the morning, Aoife was crying and the Gardaí were there. The laptop and their grey case had always been on T-07 every time I looked over during the evening.

Signed: Priya Nair
