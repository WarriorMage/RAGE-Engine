My name is Ciarán Doyle. My phone is 086 771 0342 and my badge is HB-1003. I lead Team 3, "Quokka", at HackLiffey.

I accept that I had an argument with Aoife Brennan of Team 7 at about ten past nine on Saturday night. Our team pitched a health wearable idea at a meetup in September and when I saw their band I felt they had taken it. I also thought they were taking all the sockets on the shared power strip. I said some things I should not have said. It lasted a couple of minutes and I walked away.

After that I stayed away from Team 7. I did not go back to their table at any point.

At about half twelve I went up to LAB-2 on the first floor with two of my teammates to work on our hardware. I was in LAB-2 the whole time from about 00:30 until about 02:30, soldering and testing. I did not leave. A guy from Team 7 was in there as well for part of the night and went out at some point to get food.

At around 02:30 I went to the quiet room and slept until about 07:30. When I came down there were Gardaí in the main hall and I heard that Team 7's laptop had been stolen.

I did not take anything. I was annoyed with them but I would never steal someone's work. I am happy for the Gardaí to check the LAB-2 door records, which will show I was upstairs.

Signed: Ciarán Doyle
