GARDA TECHNICAL BUREAU. CRIME SCENE EXAMINATION REPORT.
Incident ref: DMR-26-10418
Examiner: Dr. Eoin Carr, Crime Scene Examiner
Requested by: Det. Garda Sinéad Walsh
Date of examination: 2026-10-04, 10:15 to 13:40
Report date: 2026-10-05

1. SCOPE
I was asked to examine table T-07 in the main hall of the Liffey Innovation Hub, 14 Bow Street, Dublin 7, and its immediate surroundings, after the reported theft of a laptop (asset tag LT-7731) and a prototype device in a grey hard case (serial LB-PROTO-03) during the night of 2026-10-03/04.

2. SCENE DESCRIPTION
The main hall is an open-plan room of about 30 x 18 m with twelve team tables (T-01 to T-12), a low stage on the west side and a group of beanbags in front of the stage. It has two entrances: a west entrance from the main corridor and lobby, and an east entrance from the east corridor. T-07 is the table nearest the east entrance, about 4 m from the doorway. There are no internal doors or locks between the east corridor and T-07.

When I arrived, T-07 had been preserved by Gardaí since about 07:55. On the table were: a USB-C laptop charger still plugged into the shared power strip, its cable lying across the table; two energy-drink cans; a notebook with handwritten circuit notes; a soldering mat; and a phone stand. A black rucksack belonging to the victim was under the table. Inside it was an unused cable lock, still in its packaging sleeve.

3. POINT OF ENTRY
There is no sign of forced entry to the building, the main hall or any furniture. The building's external doors showed no damage. Entry was most likely by someone with lawful access, or someone admitted by a person with lawful access, or through a door released by a badge.

4. FINGERPRINTS
I examined the table surface, table edge, power strip and chair backs at T-07 with powder and an alternate light source. Many overlapping latent marks were found, as expected on a table used by many people over 20 hours. No marks were suitable for comparison in the specific area where the victim said the laptop and case had been placed (the north-east corner of the table). That area shows smearing consistent with a gloved hand or fabric being dragged across it. I cannot say when the smearing happened.

5. FIBRES
On the back of the chair at the east side of T-07 (the side nearest the east entrance), I recovered a small cluster of fibres using tape lifts, exhibit EC-04.
- Colour: dark navy blue.
- Type: polyester filament with a smooth, shiny finish.
- Assessment: consistent with the outer shell fabric of a quilted or padded jacket.
- These fibres do not match the victim's clothing, which I examined at the scene (a grey cotton hoodie and a green Team 7 cotton t-shirt).
Fibres like these are common in mass-produced outerwear. Their value depends on comparison with a specific garment. No comparison sample has been provided so far.

6. FOOTWEAR
The main hall floor is a polished concrete surface with heavy foot traffic. No useful footwear impressions were recovered in the hall. See my separate report (FOREN-002) for the east corridor and DOOR-E2.

7. LIGHTING AND SIGHT LINES
With the hall lighting at the "quiet hours" level the victim described (set by facilities at 00:30), I measured about 8 lux at T-07. In that light a person standing at T-07 would be difficult to identify from the stage area, about 20 m away, especially if they were partly lit by a phone torch. This supports the hall monitor's account that he could not tell who was at the table.

8. DEVICES
The charger left at T-07 is a genuine Lenovo 65 W USB-C unit. It was still plugged in, which suggests the laptop was unplugged at the laptop end and not by pulling the plug from the power strip. This is consistent with someone removing the laptop quickly and leaving the charger.

9. CONCLUSIONS
(a) There is no evidence of forced entry.
(b) The laptop and case were removed from the north-east corner of T-07. The area was wiped or smeared, possibly by a gloved hand or fabric.
(c) Dark navy polyester fibres consistent with a quilted jacket were recovered from the east-side chair at T-07 (exhibit EC-04). They are suitable for comparison if a garment is provided.
(d) The lighting conditions mean an eyewitness would have had difficulty identifying a person at T-07 overnight.
