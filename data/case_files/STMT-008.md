My name is Femi Adeyemi. I am a delivery driver for Slice of Dublin. My phone number is 085 664 7720. I drive a white Toyota Yaris, registration 191-D-20733, with a Slice of Dublin sign on the roof.

On the night of Saturday 3rd to Sunday 4th October I made two deliveries to the Liffey Innovation Hub on Bow Street.

The first was a big pizza order for the organisers at about twenty past midnight. I went to the front door and the security man let me in. I brought the boxes to the lobby and left after a few minutes.

The second was a small snack order, I think order number SOD-48213. The app said the customer was in the back of the building, so I drove into the rear car park at about 01:36. I rang the customer at about 01:35 when I was pulling in. A young Indian guy came out the back door to meet me, I think his name was Rohan.

While I was handing over the bags, a car came into the car park behind me and went to the far corner where it is dark. I think it was a black car, a hatchback maybe. A guy got out. He looked young, like a student, average height, wearing a dark hoodie. I did not pay much attention. It was raining and I was in a hurry because I had another order.

The customer went back inside and I drove out at about 01:42. I did not see where the other guy went.

I did not go inside the building on the second delivery. I have dash-cam but it only records when the car is moving, and it was pointing at the barrier.

Signed: Femi Adeyemi
