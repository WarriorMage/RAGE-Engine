My name is Seán Lynch, phone 086 302 7781, volunteer badge HB-0014. I was the overnight hall monitor for HackLiffey, which means I was meant to stay in the main hall, keep an eye on things and help anyone who needed it.

After quiet hours started at about half twelve most people either went upstairs or were asleep at their tables. By one o'clock there were only a few people left in the hall, mostly asleep.

I have to be honest. At about 01:18 I posted in the night-crew chat that I was going to rest my eyes on one of the beanbags by the stage. I fell asleep. I think I slept from about twenty past one until around half two.

At some point while I was half asleep I heard a zip, like a bag being zipped up, and quick footsteps on the floor near the east side of the hall, where Team 7's table is. I also saw a light moving, like a phone torch, low down near the tables. I thought it was a participant looking for a charger, which happens all the time, so I went back to sleep. If I had to guess the time I would say about quarter to two, but I did not look at my phone.

I could not tell you if it was a man or a woman. The hall lights were dimmed and I only saw the torch.

When I woke properly around half two everything seemed normal. I did not walk around the tables. I stayed in the hall until morning. I was told about the theft at around 07:10 when Aoife from Team 7 started asking everyone if they had seen her laptop.

I am very sorry that I fell asleep.

Signed: Seán Lynch
