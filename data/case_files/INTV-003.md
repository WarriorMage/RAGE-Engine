INTERVIEW TRANSCRIPT. Incident ref DMR-26-10418.
Location: Bridewell Garda Station, Interview Room 1. Start 14:30, end 15:12.
Present: Garda Paul Hennessy (PH), Rohan Mehta (RM). The interview is voluntary; RM is a witness.

PH: Rohan, thanks for coming in. I want to go through the car park in detail. First, the items. Describe the laptop.
RM: Silver ThinkPad X1 Carbon, Aoife's. It's a university one, there's a sticker with the asset tag, LT-7731. Lid had a Lumen logo sticker on it.

PH: And the prototype?
RM: The Lumen Band. Black wristband with a small sensor pod. The serial is LB-PROTO-03. It lives in a grey hard-shell case, about the size of a shoebox. It's quite distinctive, it's that ribbed grey plastic.

PH: When did you last see both items?
RM: When I left the hall at about 00:50. Both were on T-07. Laptop closed. Case shut, beside it.

PH: Was anything securing them?
RM: No. Aoife had a cable lock but we didn't use it. We had a locker, L-14, in the lobby. We didn't use that either. We were just wrecked. That's on us.

PH: Tell me about ordering the food.
RM: I ordered on the Slice of Dublin app about 01:20. Crisps, chocolate, energy drinks for the lab. The driver rang me around 01:35. He said he was at the back car park. I think the app had a pin on the back of the building.

PH: Which way did you go down?
RM: Out of LAB-2, along the landing, down the back stairs, STAIR-B. That comes out in the east corridor right beside the loading door, DOOR-E2. I pushed the green button and went out.

PH: Did you prop the door open?
RM: No. It closes by itself. It's heavy. I let it close.

PH: Describe the car park when you went out.
RM: Dark at the far end. There's a floodlight near the door and near the barrier, but the corner by the bins is pitch black. It was raining properly. The delivery car was near the door, white, with a roof sign.

PH: Tell me about the other car.
RM: It came in through the barrier while I was paying. I noticed it because it didn't park near the door, it went all the way to the dark corner by the bins. I'd say it was a dark grey saloon. A four-door, longer than a hatchback. Like an Audi or a BMW, but I'm not a car person.

PH: And the person?
RM: One person got out of the driver's door. Tall. Taller than me, and I'm five eleven. He seemed older, the way he moved, not like a student. He had a puffy jacket, the quilted kind, dark colour. I think a cap or something on his head. He stood by the car for a bit, like he was waiting.

PH: Waiting for what?
RM: I don't know. Maybe for us to leave. I didn't think about it at the time. I thought he was a sponsor coming back for something, or staff.

PH: Why a sponsor?
RM: The jacket. It looked expensive. And the sponsors were the only older people around that weekend apart from security. But I'm guessing. I really didn't see his face.

PH: The delivery driver, Femi Adeyemi, described the car as a black hatchback and the man as young, average height, in a hoodie. What do you say?
RM: I don't think so. Not a hatchback. It had a boot. And the guy was not average height, he was tall. But it was dark and wet and we were both looking at the bags, so I get why it's different.

PH: What time did you go back in?
RM: Maybe 01:41. I used my badge, HB-1008, on the reader at DOOR-E2. I made sure I heard it click shut. Then straight up the back stairs to LAB-2.

PH: Did the man in the car follow you to the door?
RM: No. When I went in he was still at the car. The door shut behind me.

PH: Did you tell anyone about him that night?
RM: No. It didn't seem important then. I only thought about it after Aoife rang me.

PH: Earlier that night, Ciarán Doyle came to your table?
RM: Yeah, about quarter past twelve. Borrowed a USB-C cable. One minute, friendly enough. He was in LAB-2 all the time I was up there, except once he went to the toilet.

PH: Who knew that the code was only on the laptop?
RM: Me and Aoife. And Aoife said she told one of the sponsors during the preview, when he asked about GitHub. I wasn't standing right there for that part.

PH: Thank you, Rohan. Interview concluded at 15:12.
