POLICE NOTE. Incident ref DMR-26-10418. Theft from the Liffey Innovation Hub, 14 Bow Street, Smithfield, Dublin 7.

Called by: Niamh Kavanagh (organiser), 07:30, 2026-10-04.
Attended: Det. Garda Sinéad Walsh and Garda Paul Hennessy, arrived 07:52.

Reported missing from table T-07 in the main hall, belonging to Team 7 (Aoife Brennan, Rohan Mehta):
1. Laptop: silver Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Carbon, Dalton University asset tag LT-7731, Lumen logo sticker on lid. Password protected. Holds the only copy of the team's source code.
2. Prototype: "Lumen Band" wristband, serial LB-PROTO-03, in a grey ribbed hard-shell case approx. 30 x 20 x 12 cm.
Approximate combined value: EUR 2,400 in hardware. The value of the code is not assessed.

Last seen: about 00:40 by Aoife Brennan and about 00:50 by Rohan Mehta. Discovered missing at about 07:05.

Scene: No sign of forced entry to the building or the main hall. The laptop charger was left plugged in at T-07. The main hall is open plan with no internal locks. The scene was preserved and the Garda Technical Bureau was requested.

Initial actions:
- Obtain CCTV for CAM-01 to CAM-05 and door access exports (facilities manager Brian Coyle).
- Obtain the badge register from the organiser.
- Statements from Team 7, the security officer Tomás Ryan, the cleaner, the hall monitor, nearby teams and sponsors.
- Note: the organiser states the main hall camera (CAM-02) was offline for a scheduled firmware update overnight.

Note by: Det. Garda Sinéad Walsh, 08:05.
