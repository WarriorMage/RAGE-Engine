My name is Gráinne O'Shea. I am a contract cleaner at the Liffey Innovation Hub. My phone is 087 903 1145 and my badge is HB-0071. My shift was midnight to six on Sunday morning.

I came in at about five past twelve through the back loading door, DOOR-E2, with my cleaning cart, like I always do.

At about half one I was going through the front lobby with my cart and I noticed the security desk was empty. Nobody was there. I thought the guard, Tomás, must have gone out for a smoke or to the toilet. It is normal enough for them to step away, so I did not say anything.

Straight after that, maybe 01:32, I mopped the east corridor, the one that goes from the back door past the back stairs to the side entrance of the main hall. That took me about five minutes and then I moved on to the toilets near the kitchen.

I did not go into the main hall because they told me there were students sleeping and working in there and to leave it until the morning.

At about a quarter past two I took the big wheeled bin out through DOOR-E2 to the skip in the car park. It was full of pizza boxes and cardboard from the hall. It was raining hard. I came back in with my badge about five minutes later. I did not see anyone in the car park. I did not see any car moving.

I finished at about five past six and left by the back door.

I never touched anything belonging to the students. The Gardaí are welcome to look in the skip.

Signed: Gráinne O'Shea
