My name is Niamh Kavanagh. I am the head organiser of HackLiffey 2026. My phone is 089 455 2016 and I hold the organiser master badge, HB-0001. My car, 202-D-11873, was in the rear car park all night.

I issued all event badges on Saturday. Participants have HB-1xxx numbers, staff and volunteers HB-00xx, and our two sponsor representatives had visitor badges HB-9041 (Dana Kowalski, TechNorth) and HB-9042 (Marcus Whelan, Brightwave Ventures). Sponsor badges were set to stay active until the end of the event at 10:00 on Sunday. That was so sponsors could bring demo kit in through the east loading door, DOOR-E2. In hindsight I should have switched them off overnight.

Dana left at around nine on Saturday evening. Marcus stayed until about 23:30. He came over to say goodnight to me in the lobby. He was wearing his navy quilted jacket, the puffer type, and his green sponsor lanyard, and he had his laptop bag over his shoulder. He said he was going back to his hotel and would be in at nine for judging.

Our volunteer Seán Lynch was the night hall monitor, staying in the main hall overnight. I went up to the organiser office on the first floor at about one o'clock to get some sleep. I posted in our night-crew chat before that to remind everyone that the main-hall camera would be off for a firmware update from one o'clock. The facilities manager had emailed us about it on Friday.

I left the office at about 06:30 and was in the kitchen when Tomás rang me at about 07:09. I came to the main hall and spoke to Aoife. I rang the Gardaí at about 07:30.

I can provide the full badge register and the door records from the facilities team.

Signed: Niamh Kavanagh
