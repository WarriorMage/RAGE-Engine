INTERVIEW TRANSCRIPT. Incident ref DMR-26-10418.
Location: Bridewell Garda Station, Interview Room 2. Start 11:00, end 11:33.
Present: Det. Garda Sinéad Walsh (SW), Garda Paul Hennessy (PH), Marcus Whelan (MW), and MW's solicitor, Ms R. Daly (RD). The interview is voluntary; MW has been cautioned.

SW: Mr Whelan, you were cautioned at the start of this interview. Do you understand the caution?
MW: I do.

SW: On 7 October you told us you drove from the hub to the Harbourview Hotel at about 23:30 and did not leave the hotel again that night. Do you stand by that?
MW: Yes.

PH: The rear car park at the hub has an automatic number plate camera at the barrier. I want to ask you about some times. What can you tell us about the car 231-D-55120 between 01:00 and 02:30 on Sunday morning?
RD: My client has already told you he was at his hotel.
MW: No comment.

PH: Did anyone else have access to the keys of that car on Saturday night?
MW: No comment.

SW: On 7 October you said you lost your visitor badge. Can you tell us where you think you lost it?
MW: I've told you. I assumed in the building or the car park.

SW: When did you first realise it was missing?
MW: Sunday morning.

SW: Did you tell anyone at the event that it was missing?
MW: I don't believe I did. There was a lot going on.

SW: Can you explain why your phone recorded no network activity between about 01:21 and 02:04 on Sunday?
MW: I may have switched it off. I was asleep.

SW: At 02:05 your phone made a call to a UK number, +44 7700 900418, that lasted just over four minutes. Who did you call?
RD: My client is not obliged to discuss private business matters.
MW: No comment.

SW: What was that call about?
MW: No comment.

SW: Is that number connected to Brightwave Ventures?
MW: No comment.

SW: At the time of that call, the phone was connected to a mast in the Smithfield area, not Grand Canal Dock. How do you explain that?
MW: No comment.

PH: Do you own a dark baseball cap?
MW: Many people do. No comment.

SW: Let's talk about the hotel. Can anyone at the Harbourview confirm you were in your room between midnight and three?
MW: I was alone. I don't imagine the night staff were watching my door.

SW: Did you use the hotel car park that night?
MW: I parked in the hotel car park, yes.

SW: Did you drive the car out of the hotel car park again after midnight?
RD: You have my client's answer on his movements.
MW: No comment.

SW: You were wearing a navy quilted jacket on Saturday. Do you still have it?
MW: I do.

SW: Would you be willing to give it to us for examination?
RD: We will consider any written request.
MW: I'll take my solicitor's advice on that.

PH: When did you return the rental car?
MW: On Monday, the 5th. At the airport, as planned.

PH: Did you clean the car or have it cleaned before you returned it?
MW: No comment.

SW: Do you know what "Find My Device" is?
MW: Of course.

SW: Were you near Hanover Quay at about 02:21 on Sunday morning?
MW: My hotel is on Hanover Quay. I was asleep in it.

SW: Mr Whelan, do you have any knowledge of where Team 7's laptop or the grey prototype case are now?
MW: No.

SW: Have you ever been in possession of either item?
MW: No.

SW: Is there anything you wish to add?
RD: My client has cooperated fully and attended voluntarily twice. He has nothing further to say at this time.

SW: Interview concluded at 11:33. Mr Whelan is free to leave. Enquiries are continuing.
