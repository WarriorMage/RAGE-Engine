My name is Marcus Whelan. I am a partner at Brightwave Ventures, which is a headline sponsor of HackLiffey 2026 and funds the Brightwave Wearables Prize. My mobile number is 087 650 2289. I am staying at the Harbourview Hotel on Hanover Quay while I am in Dublin.

I arrived at the hub at about 18:00 on Saturday. At around 20:30 I took part in the sponsor preview and visited several teams, including Team 7 and their wearable band. It is a very impressive project and I told them so. I spoke to the team lead, Aoife, for some time about their technology.

I left at about 23:30 by the front door. I said goodbye to Niamh Kavanagh, the organiser, and to the security officer at the front desk. I walked round to my rental car in the rear car park and drove straight to the Harbourview Hotel. I went to my room and went to bed. I did not leave the hotel again during the night and I did not return to the hub until about 08:55 on Sunday for judging.

When I arrived on Sunday morning the Gardaí were already there and I was told that Team 7's laptop and prototype had been stolen. That is very upsetting. Brightwave will support the team in any way we can.

I have no information about who took the items. I did not see anything suspicious while I was at the hub on Saturday evening.

I am happy to assist the Gardaí further if needed.

Signed: Marcus Whelan
