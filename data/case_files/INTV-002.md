INTERVIEW TRANSCRIPT. Incident ref DMR-26-10418.
Location: Dalton University, Student Services meeting room. Start 10:00, end 10:38.
Present: Det. Garda Sinéad Walsh (SW), Ciarán Doyle (CD). The interview is voluntary.

SW: Ciarán, you gave a statement on Sunday. I want to go over a couple of points. For the record, your badge number?
CD: HB-1003.

SW: You said that after the argument at about 21:10 you did not go back to Team 7's table at any point. Is that right?
CD: Yeah.

SW: I'm going to show you a photo. This was posted in the Team 5 group chat by Priya Nair. The chat export shows it was posted at 00:16 on Sunday. Who is the person in the red beanie standing at table T-07?
CD: (pause) That's me.

SW: So you were at Team 7's table at 00:16.
CD: Okay, yeah. I went over to borrow a USB-C cable. Ours had died and the shop was shut. Rohan gave me one. I was there for like one minute. I didn't mention it because, honestly, it was embarrassing. I'd just been shouting at them that they copied us and then I go over asking for a cable. And I knew how it would look once the laptop was gone.

SW: Did you touch the laptop or the grey case?
CD: No. The laptop was closed. The case was beside it. I didn't touch anything, I just took the cable from Rohan.

SW: Rohan confirms he lent you a cable. Priya, in her statement, said she saw you at their table "around half one, maybe a bit earlier". What do you say to that?
CD: That's wrong. At half one I was up in LAB-2. It must have been the cable thing she saw. The photo says 00:16, doesn't it?

SW: It does. Now, you also said you were in LAB-2 continuously from about 00:30 to 02:30 and you did not leave.
CD: Yeah.

SW: The LAB-2 door record shows your badge, HB-1003, entering at 00:31. It shows a push-to-exit at 01:12 and your badge entering again at 01:19. The first-floor landing camera, CAM-05, shows a man in a red beanie leaving LAB-2 at 01:12, going into the first-floor toilets, and coming back at 01:19.
CD: Oh, right. Yeah, I went to the toilet. I didn't think that counted as leaving. It's just across the landing.

SW: Did you go downstairs at any point between 00:31 and 02:34?
CD: No. Definitely not. I was soldering a board that kept failing. Ask my teammates, they were there. And Rohan from Team 7 was there most of the time too, except when he went to get food.

SW: When did Rohan leave to get food?
CD: Around half one, maybe a bit after. He came back with bags, a few minutes later. He was soaked, it was raining.

SW: Did you see anything from the LAB-2 window? Does it face the car park?
CD: It faces Bow Street, the front. I didn't see anything.

SW: Tell me about the argument with Aoife Brennan.
CD: We pitched a wearable idea at a meetup in September. When I saw their band I thought it was the same idea. I lost the head. It was stupid. I've apologised to Aoife since. I messaged her Sunday afternoon.

SW: Did anyone else know the code was only on that laptop?
CD: I didn't know that. I'd have assumed it was on GitHub like everyone else's.

SW: Did you see any sponsors around the building after midnight?
CD: No. I don't think any sponsors were there after midnight. Not that I saw.

SW: Where did you go after LAB-2?
CD: Quiet room, about half two. Slept till half seven.

SW: Thank you, Ciarán. Is there anything you want to add or correct?
CD: Just that I'm sorry I didn't mention the cable. I didn't take anything.

SW: Interview concluded at 10:38.
