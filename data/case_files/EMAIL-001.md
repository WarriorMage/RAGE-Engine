Quick heads-up for this weekend's HackLiffey event.

Our CCTV supplier has scheduled a firmware update for the main hall camera, CAM-02, during the early hours of Sunday 4 October. The update window is 01:00 to 02:00. The camera will be offline and NOT recording during that window. It may take a few minutes after 02:00 to come back online.

I tried to get the supplier to move the update to a weekday, but this was the only slot they could offer before their maintenance freeze. Sorry about the timing.

Please make sure valuables in the main hall are not left unattended overnight, especially during the update window. Lockers are available in the lobby; the organisers have the keys.

A reminder for partners bringing equipment in or out over the weekend: please use the east loading door (DOOR-E2) beside the rear car park rather than the front entrance. Your visitor badges will work on that reader. The rear car park has a barrier with number-plate recognition, so you do not need a ticket.
